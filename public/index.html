Our place

Named after jasmine.

“My café is here, half way up. Its name is Yiasemi, jasmine, the sweet-smelling flower whose white blossom shines even at midnight.”

Mariza Kouzeli, owner, from yiasemi.gr

Mnisikleous is the street of central steps that climbs from Plaka toward the Acropolis, named after the ancient architect Mnesikles. The recipes come from the plants and flavours of the Aegean islands, taught by a grandmother from the archipelago.

A small guide

How to order Greek coffee.

The sugar goes into the briki while it brews, so you choose before it is made.

  • Σκέτος[ské-tos]

    No sugar. Strong and bitter.

  • Μέτριος[mé-tri-os]

    Medium sweet, with one teaspoon of sugar.

  • Γλυκός[gli-kós]

    Sweet, with two teaspoons of sugar.

Tip: let the grounds settle for a minute and leave the last sip in the cup.

Good to know

Questions guests ask.

Where is Yiasemi?

At Mnisikleous 31, Plaka, 105 55 Athens, half way up the stepped Mnisikleous Street that climbs from Plaka toward the Acropolis.

What is the nearest metro station?

Monastiraki (lines 1 and 3) is the closest station; from there it is a short walk through Plaka.

Are there vegetarian and vegan options?

Yes. Most of the food menu is vegetarian and several dishes are vegan; they are marked on the menu.

I have a food allergy. What should I do?

Please tell the staff before you order. They can explain the ingredients of each dish.

Is the café step-free?

No. Mnisikleous is a stepped street and the café is half way up, so the approach involves stairs.
